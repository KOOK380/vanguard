# Open facts

A running list of facts we couldn't verify, and the neutral wording used instead. Add new entries as they come up. If a fact is confirmed later, record the source and date in its row before changing the site.

"Neutral wording" is the text the site uses. "Nothing" means the topic is left out altogether.

## How we treat the current site

The current site's facts are our starting truth (decision 41). A fact on it can be used as stated unless it is contradicted, looks like template leftover, or is a claim of rank or opinion. The full record is in [current-site.md](current-site.md).

Only facts with a source URL in [research.md](research.md) go on the site (decision 55). Being on the company's own site doesn't make a fact independently confirmed. As of 2026-11-03 we had read all eight URLs in the site's sitemap, archived copies, and the domain record, and run web searches on the company name, address, domain and named staff.

- **Found:** no independent source for this company, such as a directory listing, news item, LinkedIn page or registry entry.
- **Not checked:** direct queries to official licence and supplier registers, phone calls, emails or site visits. A failed search doesn't prove a claim is false.
