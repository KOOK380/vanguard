### Technical and accessibility problems

- **Headings:** no page has an H1.
- **Alt text:** missing or meaningless on most images, such as "", "Title", "2", or raw filenames.
- **Meta descriptions:** auto-generated from page text, including menu words such as "About Us Home About Us".
- **Nested page:** the Clients & Groups HTML contains a second `<title>` and `<html lang>` from the embedded hotel section.
- **Oversized images:** two hotel PNGs of about 2.1 MB and 2.5 MB, and The Park logo declared at 7500 × 7500 px.
- **External image hosting:** the three hotel images load from `i.postimg.cc`, a third-party host the site doesn't control.
- **Possible AI image:** The Oriental Hotel's filename is `make-this-full-view-and-202605191618-(1).jpg`, which looks like an image-generator prompt.
- **Page weight:** the home page made 67 requests (20 by scripts) for 814 KB after 3 seconds. Contact made 60 requests for 1,308 KB, but this is a rough figure from one visit, with the browser pane possibly in the background.
